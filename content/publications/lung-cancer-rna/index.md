abstract: >
  We report the discovery of RNA biomarkers for early-stage lung cancer detection
  from patients' blood biopsy. Using small non-coding RNA profiling from
  next-generation sequencing, the approach achieves an AUCROC of 0.76 at Stage I
  and 0.81 at Stage IV, demonstrating the potential of liquid biopsy-based
  diagnostics for early lung cancer detection.

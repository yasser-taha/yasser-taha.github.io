abstract: >
  We propose a Graph Convolutional Network (GCN)-based approach for pharmaceutical
  cross-selling recommendations that exploits the structural relations between
  products and their ingredients. By devising a novel method to re-rank edge weights
  in the cross-selling graph, we overcome popularity bias in retail recommendations.
  The system is deployed in production at Pharmatechnic, demonstrating real-world
  impact at scale.

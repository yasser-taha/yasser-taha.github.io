abstract: >
  Small non-coding RNAs (sncRNAs) are a diverse class of regulatory molecules
  whose functional annotation remains challenging due to the high fragmentation
  and ambiguity present in next-generation sequencing data. We introduce
  TransfoRNA, an explainable machine learning framework that maps small non-coding
  RNA NGS fragments back to their parental precursors, shedding light on their
  functional role and involvement in disease mechanisms. TransfoRNA leverages
  Transformer-based architectures combined with contrastive learning and
  multimodal inputs to deliver robust, interpretable annotations even under
  highly uncertain conditions.

summary: >
  TransfoRNA is an explainable ML framework using Transformers and contrastive
  learning to map small non-coding RNA fragments to their parental precursors,
  enabling functional annotation under high biological uncertainty.
